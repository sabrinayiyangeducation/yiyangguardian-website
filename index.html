About us

关于易扬

易扬教育监护服务英国萨里郡、伯克郡与汉普郡，为就读英国寄宿学校的国际学生提供教育监护与照顾服务。我们是独立监护人机构：与您初次见面的人，就是照顾您孩子的人。

Yiyang Education & Guardianship is an educational guardianship service covering Surrey, Berkshire and Hampshire, providing guardianship and care for international students attending boarding schools in the United Kingdom. We are a sole guardian practice: the person who meets your family is the person who looks after your child.
